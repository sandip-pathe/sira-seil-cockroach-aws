MEETAI PRO — INTEGRATION CAPABILITIES
Product: MeetAI Pro
Seller: MeetAI Inc.
Document Version: 1.0
Date: August 2026

SECTION 1: CRM INTEGRATIONS

MeetAI Pro supports the following CRM integrations:

Standard Tier ($80/seat/month):
- Salesforce (native)
- Pipedrive (native)
- Zoho CRM (native)

HubSpot Integration Tier ($120/seat/month):
- All Standard integrations PLUS
- HubSpot CRM (native sync)
- HubSpot Sales Hub (activity logging)

IMPORTANT LIMITATION: HubSpot CRM integration is available ONLY on the
HubSpot Integration tier at $120/seat/month. The Standard tier at
$80/seat/month does NOT include HubSpot connectivity. There is no
add-on or workaround to enable HubSpot on the Standard tier.

SECTION 2: MEETING CAPABILITIES

- AI-powered transcription (all tiers)
- Action item extraction (all tiers)
- Speaker identification (all tiers)
- Real-time summaries (all tiers)
- Calendar sync: Google Workspace, Outlook 365

SECTION 3: DATA RESIDENCY

All data stored in US-EAST-1 (Virginia) by default.
EU residency available on Enterprise tier only.
