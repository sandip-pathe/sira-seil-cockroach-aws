# First category and release boundary

Decision date: 2026-10-21. This is an evidence-backed product-scope proposal from repository contracts, not evidence of customer demand. Validate commercial ownership and any real first-release cohort before treating it as a launch commitment.

## Proposed slice

**Meeting intelligence / meeting assistant SaaS** for a professional-services team evaluating documented meeting-note, conferencing, retention, residency and CRM-fit evidence. The repository's retrieval fixture already spans Granola, Fathom, tl;dv, Fireflies and a decoy with five corresponding evidence queries. This makes it the narrowest category with explicit evaluation examples.

## Supported path and boundaries

1. Create and refine a buyer need/brief with typed company context and constraints.
2. Search the persistent catalog and, when needed, conduct public-web product research with source links and freshness labels.
3. Compare product evidence and mark gaps or stale claims. Public research is not seller attestation.
4. A seller can create a private product draft, attach/review evidence, and explicitly publish an allowlisted version; suspension withdraws the catalog projection.
5. Where qualified engagement and offer contracts apply, keep disclosure/consent, seller response, exact offer, buyer approval and purchase intent as separate durable actions.
6. A supported commercial handoff is an exact, human-operated external HTTPS step. Record only the handoff lifecycle and attributed outcome checkpoint supported by the contract; do not claim the platform charged, activated or fulfilled the purchase.

## Evidence and money contract check

- **Evaluation:** five meeting-software retrieval queries plus deterministic five-case qualification grounding evaluator. The latter is a boundary test; live provider quality remains unverified.
- **Evidence:** versioned public observations with source URLs, observed times, expiry and seller evidence publication; source membership does not prove assertion truth.
- **Money:** exact purchase-intent and payment-handoff domain contracts include amount/currency/recipient/reference and expiry. Payment provider result and entitlement are expressly outside the platform contract.
- **Offer/approval:** offer negotiation and approval-request/purchase-intent routes exist. Their complete live tenant journey was not exercised for this audit.
- **Handoff:** external human-operated handoff route exists; external completion is not inferred from opening the link.

## Out of scope for this first complete experience

Automated payment or checkout, fulfillment/entitlement, renewal/cancellation, SaaS provisioning, identity/admin grants, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 synchronization, Slack/Teams notifications, API-key issuance, MCP/skill installation or execution, internal-tool deployment, and goods procurement. These require category-specific evidence, permission, money, failure/recovery and outcome contracts.

## Next category after this slice

API subscriptions are the next candidate because cost meters, included usage, overage, service regions and reliability evidence require a distinct evaluation contract. Do not reuse a seat-based SaaS total-cost model until API usage assumptions and exact totals are defined.
