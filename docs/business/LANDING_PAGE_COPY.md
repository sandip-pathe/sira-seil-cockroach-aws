## SIRA

# An AI buying agent that works for your company.

Tell SIRA what you are trying to solve. It learns about your team, existing tools, priorities, constraints, budget, and approval process. Then it finds relevant products and speaks to their SEILs for you.

You get a short list built around your company, not a generic ranking.

**Action:** Talk to SIRA

## SEIL

### Meet SEIL

# An AI product agent that represents what you sell.

SEIL learns your positioning, jobs to be done, capabilities, limitations, evidence, pricing, and terms. It checks seller claims against documentation, the web, reviews, and approved company sources.

When SIRA asks, SEIL makes the strongest honest case—or says `PASS` when the product is wrong for that buyer.

**Action:** Build your SEIL

## How it works

# Two agents. One qualified purchase.

1. **SIRA understands the buyer.** It turns private company context into a requirement brief containing only what may be shared.
2. **SEIL understands the product.** It responds with relevant evidence, limits, questions, pricing, and terms.
3. **They compare notes.** SIRA decides for the buyer; SEIL never controls the ranking.
4. **The buyer approves.** Prava gives the exact approved purchase bounded payment authority.

## Example

# The cheapest option is not always the right match.

A client-services company asks SIRA for meeting-intelligence software. One product cannot support its client-data policy, so its SEIL returns `PASS`. Another proves the required workflow, explains the trade-offs, and submits an offer. SIRA recommends it because it fits this company—not because the seller paid to be seen.

## Trust

# Each agent has one loyalty.

- SIRA protects the buyer's private context and decision.
- SEIL represents the product but cannot buy rank.
- Claims, independent evidence, limits, and unknowns stay distinct.
- Sellers see only what the buyer permits for that interaction.
